HOW TO PLAY

GO: Hold UP / SPACE, or press-and-hold the road — your car accelerates while you hold.
STEER: LEFT / RIGHT arrows (or A / D), or drag your finger / mouse toward the side you want.
SPINNING BARRIERS: striped bars that sweep the road — hitting one costs a wrench and slows you down.
RAMPS: yellow chevron pads — drive over them for a speed boost + 50 pts.
RIVALS: 3 rival cars per race. Overtake one for +100 pts. They get faster every race.
OFF-ROAD: grass slows you down — stay on the asphalt.
WIN A RACE: reach the chequered line before the 60-second clock runs out.
WRENCHES: you have 3 per race. Lose all 3 and the race is over — retry instantly.
PAUSE: P or the ❚❚ button. MUTE: M or the speaker button.
